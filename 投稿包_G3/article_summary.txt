This Investigation compares two sequence models that predict whether an Arabidopsis thaliana gene sits above or below median tissue-averaged expression from a 6 kb window. On a locked public split, a low-rank-adapted AgroNT checkpoint outperforms a compact convolutional network by about one tenth in receiver-operating-characteristic area. Complementary tests ask which annotated flanks carry the signal and whether the same ranking holds when Arabidopsis checkpoints are applied to four crop test sets. The intended readers are plant geneticists who need a reproducible performance and cost reference before choosing a convolutional or foundation-model scorer. Fine-tuned weights are reproduced by retraining rather than downloaded.
