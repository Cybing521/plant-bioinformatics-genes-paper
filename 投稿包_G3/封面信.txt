Dear Editor,

Please consider this Investigation for G3: Genes|Genomes|Genetics.

The manuscript reports a locked comparison, in Arabidopsis thaliana, of a compact DeepSEA-style convolutional network with AgroNT fine-tuned by low-rank adaptation. Both models predict tissue-averaged high-versus-low gene expression from public Plants Genomic Benchmark sequence windows. Complementary tests locate predictive signal in annotated flanks and ask whether the Arabidopsis ranking holds on independent rice, maize, tomato, and soybean test sets.

G3 publishes statistical and computational methods that generate useful genetic information. This study is a reproducible performance-and-cost reference for plant geneticists choosing a sequence-to-expression scorer. It does not include wet-lab validation and does not claim a causal cis-regulatory atlas.

Suggested article type: Investigation.
